YOUR DATA & YOUR CHOICES

Privacy policy

GachaTime · Draft updated October 3, 2026

Draft for review. This document describes the current development version. The publisher’s legal identity, privacy contact, audience, launch regions, and online-data retention must be confirmed before this becomes the published policy.

GachaTime stores focus sessions, timer deadlines, reward history, points, and preferences on your device. Optional online features and third-party services process additional information as described below.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes GachaTime, presented under the Sigani Studios name, and this project website. It explains the current development implementation. Accounts, cloud backup, production billing, and live rewarded advertising are not enabled in that version.

Publisher and contact: the legal publisher name and a working privacy email are pending confirmation. Do not send personal information through LinkedIn or public project comments.

2. Information stored on your device

GachaTime saves your current session and deadlines, completed focus count, reward and spin history, points balance, selected skins, timer and display preferences, and notification settings. Optional leaderboard participation also stores an installation identifier and authentication secret locally.

This information keeps timers and rewards consistent across app restarts. GachaTime does not currently monitor the use of other apps or block them. Core focus and reward state is stored locally rather than uploaded for cloud backup.

Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove local progress and settings. This does not by itself delete information already sent to an online service. Device and operating-system backup behavior may differ; deletion from every backup is not promised.

3. Optional leaderboard

The prerelease leaderboard connects only when a server has been configured and you choose to participate. It uses an anonymous installation identifier, a credential used to authenticate that installation, and your cumulative points. An anonymous display name, score, and ranking can be visible to other leaderboard participants. Your authentication credential is not a public leaderboard field.

The hosting provider and server may also process IP addresses, request times, and other technical request information needed to operate and secure the service. The server’s hosting provider, retention schedule, and deletion-request procedure must be documented before public deployment.

The current prototype has no recoverable user account. Clearing local data may prevent you from accessing the same leaderboard identity without deleting the old server record.

4. GachaTime+ and RevenueCat

GachaTime uses RevenueCat to manage purchase status and access to GachaTime+. RevenueCat processes an app-user identifier and purchase-related information such as transactions, product identifiers, and entitlement status. Its SDK may also process technical information described in its privacy policy.

The current integration uses RevenueCat’s debug Test Store; production store payments are not enabled. For a production release, the app-store purchase flow and the actual SDK configuration must be reflected here. GachaTime does not receive your full payment-card details from RevenueCat.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy. RevenueCat explains the purchase data disclosed for app-store privacy reporting in its App Privacy documentation.

5. Rewarded ads

The default development experience uses a simulated ad. An optional debug configuration uses Google Mobile Ads test inventory. Simulated ads do not make advertising requests; Google SDK test mode should not be interpreted as a guarantee that no information is processed.

When the Google advertising SDK is used, its processing can include IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information depending on platform, consent, and configuration. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure and privacy policy.

Live advertising is not enabled in the current app. The production consent flow, audience settings, and privacy controls must be finalized before enabling it. Rewarded ads are optional; you may take your eligible break without choosing an additional ad reward.

6. Notifications and permissions

On supported Android devices, GachaTime can request notification permission to show break countdowns and completion alerts. Precise alarms may require separate system access. You control these permissions through device settings, including whether notifications are visible on the lock screen.

The optional foreground keep-screen-on setting helps prevent automatic sleep while focusing. It does not monitor other apps.

7. This website

This website does not include analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms, embedded social widgets, or cookies set by the site’s own code.

Azure Static Web Apps hosts the site. Serving pages necessarily involves processing requests, including technical connection information. Microsoft’s handling is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service covered by its own privacy practices.

8. Why information is used and shared

Information is used to run timers, save progress, provide optional rankings, manage purchase access, deliver optional rewarded ads, and operate and secure online services. Public leaderboard data is shared with leaderboard viewers; service providers process information needed for their functions.

GachaTime’s current implementation does not contain a developer-operated personal-data marketplace. The final policy must separately address any advertising-related sharing, applicable legal bases, international processing, and legally required disclosures for the launch regions.

9. Retention, security, and your choices

Local app information remains until it is changed, cleared, or removed, subject to operating-system behavior. RevenueCat, Google, and hosting services apply their own retention practices. A specific server retention period has not yet been set for the optional leaderboard.

You can choose not to participate in the leaderboard, skip optional rewarded ads, and change device permissions. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain your personal information, or object to certain processing. A working privacy contact and request-handling process must be added before publication.

Security measures reduce risk, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy does not promise deletion of purchase records that a store or provider must retain.

10. Audience and policy changes

The intended audience, minimum age if applicable, and launch countries remain to be confirmed. Child-directed use and related SDK settings must be assessed before public release.

Material changes to the app’s data practices will require an updated policy and any notices or consent required for the release. The final published version will show its effective date here.
